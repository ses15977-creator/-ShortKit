# Shortkit integrated completion pack

Added on top of v15:
- Brand intro/outro overlays
- CTA presets and custom CTA text
- Automatic BGM ducking during narrated scenes
- Transition styles: cut, fade, slide, zoom
- Saved-project duplication
- Export presets for Shorts, Reels, TikTok, and WebM archive
- Compatibility defaults for older projects

No new AI API is required for these features.

Compatibility: existing brand and personal templates without the new fields continue to use safe defaults. Built-in templates now choose a transition style as part of the preset.
